# Bloom Typography

## Display and headings

Font: Newsreader

Used for:
- major page titles
- hero statements
- selected reflective quotes
- Bloom wordmark exploration

## Interface and body

Font: Atkinson Hyperlegible

Used for:
- navigation
- buttons
- form labels
- routine steps
- focus controls
- helper text
- accessibility settings

## Rules

- Body text must remain clear and undecorated.
- Avoid long italic passages.
- Do not use the display font for small controls.
- Preserve comfortable line height and spacing.
- Users may switch to OpenDyslexic through Page Controls.
