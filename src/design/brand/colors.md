- Forest: `#355B3E`
- Deep Forest: `#24452F`
- Sage: `#7E9D73`
- Soft Sage: `#DCE7D7`
- Cream: `#FAF7F1`
- Warm White: `#FFFDFC`
- Chamomile: `#F2D8A7`
- Soft Peach: `#E8B6A3`
- Terracotta: `#B96F55`
- Primary Text: `#293A2E`
- Secondary Text: `#667269`
- Border: `#D8E0D6`

## Accessibility rule

Colours must not communicate meaning on their own. Text and interactive
elements must retain sufficient contrast in every Bloom theme.
